    Theme colour

    Draws a bar on the step in progress that drains over its last 10 seconds.

    Show

    Hides the build name and clock (header) and the status and hint (footer). Turning one off makes the window that much smaller. The header is the handle you drag the window by, so it comes back for a moment whenever you unlock.

    Size grows the whole window; Step text grows only the build list. Growing just the list leaves the window as wide as it was, so long steps get cut off with … — widen it with Width when that happens.

    Position

    To place it by hand, unlock with Ctrl+Alt+L and drag the header.

    Sound

    Sound Built-in default

    It only fires in auto mode, when the step advances. Short sounds work best — WAV · MP3 · OGG · M4A · FLAC, up to 5MB.
